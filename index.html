About Me

Hi, I'm Niamh Fennessy

For over sixteen years, I've had the priviliege of supporting people through some of lifes most challenging and transformative moments. I feel honoured to walk alongside people as they discover new ways of understanding themselves.

I approach therapy with calmness, compassion, and reflective curiosity, creating a space where you can feel genuinely seen, heard, and accepted.

Therapy Built on

Compassion, Curiosity & Connection

At the heart of work is the belief that meaningful change begins when we feel genuinely heard, understood, and accepted.

Rooted in person-centered counselling. I create the conditions where your own insight and inner wisdom can emerege. Together we explore your experiences with openness and kindness, allowing change to unfold in a way that feels right for you.

Therapy isn't about becoming someone different. It's about creating the space where more of who you truly are can gently emerge.
